Refresh and redirect demo pages

These pages show what automatic page refreshes and redirects are like for users, before and after the fix.

Automatic refresh

  • Waitlist page with an automatic refresh
    Reloads itself every 30 seconds. Seat counts and the "last updated" time change on each reload, and anything typed in the form is erased.
  • Waitlist page, fixed
    No automatic refresh. Seat counts update only when you select "Check for open seats."

Automatic redirect

  • Waiting room with an automatic redirect
    Sends you to the registration form 20 seconds after the page loads, without warning.
  • Waiting room, fixed
    After 20 seconds, announces "It's your turn" and shows a link. You choose when to continue.
