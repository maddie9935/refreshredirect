Before you register

  1. Have your list of course numbers ready, including a few backup choices in case your first choices are full.
  2. Check that you've met the prerequisites for each course. Courses with unmet prerequisites can't be added.
  3. Make sure your courses don't overlap. The system won't let you register for two courses that meet at the same time.
  4. If a course is full, you can join its waitlist. You'll be notified by email if a seat opens.
  5. Once your window opens, you have 15 minutes to complete your registration before your spot passes to the next student.
